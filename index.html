ParcelForge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

ParcelForge ("the game") is a puzzle game for Android published by EasyKit Labs. This policy explains what information is used and why.

Information the game stores on your device

  • Game progress and settings are stored only on your device: unlocked levels, stars, scores and completed levels, your hint count, which tutorials you have seen, and your sound, music and vibration choices.
  • The game also stores the time you first played and a few ad counters (completed levels and retries since ads became possible, and when the last full-screen ad was shown). These are used only to decide when an ad may be shown.
  • There is no account, no login and no game server. This data is not sent to EasyKit Labs, and the game does not use analytics, crash-reporting or other tracking services of its own.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

ParcelForge is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, a third-party service provided by Google. Ads are not shown during the first 24 hours after you first play a level; this is how the game schedules ads, not a privacy setting.

  • Google AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and your interactions with ads, to show, measure and improve ads, to prevent fraud and keep the service secure, and, where you allow it, to personalise ads.
  • Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads; the exact menu depends on your device).

Consent and privacy options

  • Before any ad is requested, the game uses Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform). Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), you are asked for your choice, which can affect whether ads are personalised. No ads are requested until you have made your choice in that consent request, or until Google's consent tool reports that no choice is needed where you are.
  • Where these privacy choices apply to you, a Privacy options button appears in the game's Settings screen so you can review or change your choice at any time.

Time check

To apply the 24-hour rule reliably, the game may read the current time from Google's public connectivity-check address (www.gstatic.com). This is a standard web request that only reads the time; no game progress or other game data is included in it. Like any web request, it reaches Google's server from your device's IP address.

Children

ParcelForge is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: for ads and the time check. The game itself works fully offline.
  • Vibration: optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • The Google Mobile Ads SDK adds the advertising ID permission and a few other standard permissions it needs. None of them asks you for access to your files, location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Data retention and deletion

  • ParcelForge does not have user accounts, and EasyKit Labs keeps no server-side copy of your game data.
  • Settings → Reset progress in the game erases your level progress (your settings and the ad timing are kept).
  • Your game data stays on your device until you clear the app's data (Android Settings → Apps → ParcelForge → Storage) or uninstall the game; either removes it. The game opts out of Android's automatic backup (allowBackup=false), so this data is not copied to your Google account backup; on Android 12 and later, a direct device-to-device transfer may still copy it.
  • Information processed by Google for advertising is handled under Google's own policies and retention practices (see the links above).
